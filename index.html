ROSIE • PHASE GUIDE
Hours 0–8 • Know What You’re Working With

1. Shift from reaction to action

Job loss or income disruption can create urgency. Give yourself room to process what happened, then choose a concrete point when you will shift into action mode.

2. Skills inventory

Identify the strengths and accomplishments you can put to work immediately. Start with three skills and two results you are proud of.

3. Network audit

You are not asking these people for a job. You are identifying people who may offer perspective, introductions, employer knowledge, or encouragement.

4. What carries forward from BOOST?

Use the evidence you already built. You do not need to restart your career thinking from zero.

Hours 8–20 • Get Ready to Be Seen

1. The 30-minute résumé fix

Turn one responsibility into an accomplishment using the deck’s core structure: Action + What You Did + Measurable Result.

Preview: Add the three pieces above to build an accomplishment statement.

2. Digital presence quick check

Make sure the professional story employers find online supports the story in your résumé.

3. Target employers

Stop treating the entire internet as your job search. Name three employers worth investigating first.

4. Strategic research checklist

For each target employer, look beyond the posting before you apply.

Phase 3 — The Outreach Blitz

This is where preparation becomes movement: people, targeted opportunities, and specific asks.

ROSIE • PHASE GUIDE
Hours 20–36 • Make the Search Active

1. Quick decision: what is the strongest outreach?

You contact a former supervisor who knows your work well. Which opening is strongest?

Choose the option that asks for perspective and makes it easy for the other person to help.

2. Build one outreach message

Keep the situation brief. Name your target. Ask for perspective or a short conversation rather than putting someone on the spot for a job.

Draft: Add the fields above and I’ll assemble a starting message.

3. Strategic applications

Use quality over volume. Apply where the role and employer make sense, then connect the application to human outreach when possible.

4. Recruiter / staffing outreach

Add one recruiter, staffing firm, or workforce contact that may have access to opportunities beyond the postings you see yourself.

Phase 4 — Follow-Up & Systems

The first 48 hours create momentum. Your system keeps that momentum from disappearing next week.

Not complete
ROSIE • PHASE GUIDE
Hours 36–48 • Build a System

1. Create the first row of your tracker

Track the company, role, contact, follow-up date, and status so opportunities do not disappear into memory.

2. Follow-up habit

Everyone who gives you time or useful information should get a brief, personalized thank-you.

3. Build a daily routine

The deck recommends focused time for networking, applications, and skill-building. Choose the version you can actually sustain.

4. Set weekly process goals

Track actions you control, not only outcomes you cannot control.
